# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Book Exchange serves readers who want a simple way to catalog their books, share copies temporarily, give books permanently, and close returns. The public Landing page is a brand and marketing surface for first-time or returning visitors deciding whether the exchange feels trustworthy and worth joining. The authenticated product interface is a task surface for signed-in readers and admins managing shelves, holds, handoffs, returns, profiles, settings, and recovery operations.

## Product Purpose

Book Exchange exists to make person-to-person book movement legible: what a reader owns, what is held, who has a copy, and what action should happen next. Success means the Landing page makes the service feel calm, premium, book-focused, and memorable, while the authenticated app stays efficient, predictable, and clear during catalog and exchange workflows.

## Brand Personality

Calm, cinematic, and archival. The brand should feel like a dark editorial library with warm paper, copper detail, careful typography, and a serious respect for books as physical objects. It should feel premium without becoming cold, technically impressive without showing off, and detailed without becoming cluttered.

## Anti-references

Do not make the brand playful, cartoonish, neon, fantasy, or generic SaaS. Avoid excessive cards, nested boxes, decorative clutter, loud gradients, stock startup dashboards, mascot-like illustration, and template landing-page scaffolding. The authenticated product should not inherit cinematic Landing effects that slow down routine work.

## Design Principles

Books are the subject. Every strong brand move should make the physicality, exchange, or care of books more vivid.

Atmosphere before decoration. Premium means controlled pacing, texture, contrast, and restraint, not more ornaments.

One surface, two registers. The Landing page can be cinematic and story-led; the signed-in app must remain stable, task-first, and familiar.

Show the system through behavior. Technical ambition should appear through smooth interaction, responsive structure, accessibility, and a future 3D scroll story that remains usable.

Preserve trust. Exchange flows handle ownership, holds, and admin actions, so copy, layout, and states must stay clear and never theatrical at the point of action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, live regions for async states, and reduced-motion alternatives. The future scroll-driven Landing story must not gate content behind animation, must be navigable without pointer precision, and must keep all core messaging available when reduced motion is enabled. Internationalized copy can change length significantly, so layouts must tolerate longer labels without clipping.
